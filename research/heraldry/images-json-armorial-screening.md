# Two outward-facing swan heads in the armorial images

## Corrected result

**Yes: the image inventory contains two direct matches, and they are the two entries with a region named `goses`.** My earlier screening missed them because I did not use that region metadata to locate the relevant shield details; its “no matches” conclusion was wrong and is superseded here.

Both shields bear a pair of long-necked bird heads turned outward, away from each other. The Cod.icon. 309 example is colored and has a blue field. The Cod.icon. 307 example is monochrome and its inscription/bearer is not securely read here. In each image the paired heads are charges on the shield, while a separate bird device appears in the crest.
