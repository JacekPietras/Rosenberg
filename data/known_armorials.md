# Known armorials

Armorials containing arms of German medieval families, including works with broader geographical coverage. Dates refer to the armorial or surviving manuscript where known. Later compilations and related heraldic registers are included where they document these families. Dashes indicate information not yet located. “Not checked” means Liebenstein coverage remains unverified. Some entries are candidates from broader heraldic surveys, including chronicles, festival books and genealogies; their heraldic or German family content still needs verification as noted in each row.

## Liebenstein name variants for searching

Search **Liebenstein, Liebenstain, Liebensteyn, Libenstein, Liebinstein, Livenstain, Lybenstein, Lybinstein**, and the long-*s* form **Liebenſtein**; also try **von/de Liebenstein** and **Liebensteiner**. **Lebenstein** is a cautious variant. Some spellings in the list attest place names or distinct branches, so a match alone does not identify a lineage.
